A closer look

PCx needed a way to make career progression and daily work understandable in the same product. I worked through the requirements and developed an AI-assisted frontend prototype to make the proposed behavior tangible.

Begin with the disagreements.

The brief used different stage names and completion rules in different places. It also treated “Productive Agent” as both a career stage and a production level. I kept those conflicts separate from design proposals rather than quietly combining them.

Keep the rule beside the result.

The daily view puts urgency before priority. In the demo, an overdue medium-priority task appears ahead of high-priority work due today. “Why is this first?” explains the rule in place.

Three surfaces, one prototype

01 Agent day 02 Verification 03 Path builder

Interface reconstructions from the original frontend prototype · mock data

Make the limits as clear as the idea.

The delivered assessment was an AI-assisted frontend prototype using mock data, with no backend. The accompanying architecture and roadmap described proposed next steps. The prototype was delivered for discussion, rather than launched as a production platform.
