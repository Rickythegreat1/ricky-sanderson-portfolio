02 / Nexus · Internal interview synthesis

Join the conversation.
Then find the words.

Finding

The interview synthesis identified question formulation as a barrier: people may need help before they know how to phrase the question.

Recommendation

Let people join a table first, so seeking help does not depend on composing a precise question. Use this table-first entry instead of asking through a search bar.

The 2018 interview synthesis records this recommendation. It does not establish a shipped change or measured improvement.

How I structured and followed up the research +

Task protocol

Follow a learning session.

I defined paired scenarios for joining a table, solving questions, visiting profiles and finding a prior question in the knowledge base. The archived protocol records tasks, not task-success results.

Interview synthesis

Keep findings tied to context.

Four participant-specific internal interview summaries cover whiteboard tools, graphs, text controls and navigation. Four is the number of summaries located, not a claim about the total research sample.

Measurement planning

Turn questions into measures.

For Element, I connected questions and hypotheses to proposed measures: schedule changes, calendar use, issue-resolution time and CSV downloads. These are measurement plans, not reported improvements.
