# Clay-inspired portfolio direction

## Basis

The reference is Clay, the UX design and branding agency at https://clay.global/, not the unrelated Clay sales-data company. Its homepage, work/case presentation and agency presentation were visually inspected for this edition. No official public brand manual was located. These are inferred website guidelines, not Clay-approved rules.

Ricky's identity remains primary. “Selected for Clay · Contract UX Researcher” identifies the application audience; it does not claim employment, partnership or endorsement. Clay's logo and proprietary font are not reproduced.

## Visual language applied

- White and near-black editorial shell, with original project imagery supplying color.
- Strong sans-serif display headings, tight tracking, short line lengths and generous negative space.
- Compact black pill for contact, restrained neutral secondary actions, clear focus treatment.
- Large, accurately labeled work artifacts and a simple grid that becomes one column on narrow screens.
- Charcoal closing section with white type, inspired by the agency's contrasting About treatment.
- No universal purple or other project-specific color lifted from an individual Clay case study.

Working palette: ink `#06070A`, charcoal `#171921`, white `#FFFFFF`, light neutral surfaces and muted secondary gray. The source portfolio's locally bundled Nimbus Sans is the licensed substitute for the reference site's proprietary typography. The font license is retained under `assets/`.

## Choreography chosen for this portfolio

These durations are design decisions for this edition, not claims about Clay's implementation: press 120 ms, feedback 180 ms, surfaces and selection 260 ms, exit 160 ms. Main-page entrances and image hover treatments are deliberately slower and low-amplitude.

- Immediate focus, selection and reading access. Reading surfaces do not overlap in crossfades. Cross-document snapshot transitions are replaced by a local heading entrance.
- Reading content stays still during scroll entry and gallery selection. Subtle rule and surface cues replace content movement.
- Optional disclosures reveal readable content immediately, with a short summary cue and rotating state indicator; video playback remains user-controlled.
- Gallery selection, image inspection, zoom, close/reopen and local demo behavior retained.
- Reduced-motion rules keep content visible and minimize transitions.
